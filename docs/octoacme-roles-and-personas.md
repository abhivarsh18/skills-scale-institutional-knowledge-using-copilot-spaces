# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and execute testing strategies to ensure deliverables meet quality standards. They establish quality gates, oversee test execution, and have authority to block releases if quality thresholds are not met.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy and testing standards for projects
- Establish and maintain quality gates and acceptance criteria for releases
- Oversee test execution, including unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Identify, document, and track defects through resolution
- Approve quality sign-off before release to production
- Collaborate on test planning and strategy definition

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables that meet or exceed quality standards
- Reduce defects in production and post-release issues
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage and traceability
- Minimize rework and quality-related delays

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy and QA planning sessions
- Defect tracking and status reports
- Quality gate reviews and release sign-off meetings
- Collaboration with developers on test case design and execution

### Cross-functional Interactions
- **With Developers**: Collaborates on test planning, test case design, and defect resolution
- **With Product Managers**: Aligns on acceptance criteria and quality expectations for features
- **With Project Managers**: Reports on quality metrics and test coverage status
- **With Security Stakeholder**: Coordinates security testing and penetration testing activities

---

## Security Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Security Stakeholders ensure that projects incorporate security best practices from design through deployment. They have authority to veto deployments if security vulnerabilities are identified.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements during project initiation and design phases
- Conduct threat assessments and identify potential security risks
- Define security testing requirements and standards
- Perform or oversee security testing and vulnerability assessments
- Authorize deployment from a security perspective
- Establish security controls and mitigation strategies

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and risk exposure
- Ensure secure-by-design principles are applied to all deliverables
- Maintain compliance with security policies and standards
- Enable rapid response to security incidents and threats

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during design and architecture phases
- Threat assessment and risk documentation
- Security testing reports and vulnerability findings
- Security sign-off on releases and deployments

### Cross-functional Interactions
- **With Architects/Developers**: Collaborates on secure design principles and code review for security
- **With QA Lead**: Coordinates security test cases and penetration testing
- **With Compliance Officer**: Aligns on security requirements that support compliance obligations
- **With Project Managers**: Reports on security risks and mitigation timelines

---

## Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance Officers ensure that projects adhere to regulatory and organizational compliance requirements. They audit processes for compliance, document compliance evidence, and have authority to approve process changes affecting compliance posture.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable regulatory and compliance requirements for projects
- Audit project processes and deliverables for compliance adherence
- Document compliance evidence and maintain audit trails
- Identify compliance risks and recommend mitigations
- Approve process changes that affect compliance posture
- Coordinate with security and quality functions on compliance alignment

### Goals
- Ensure all projects maintain compliance with relevant regulations
- Reduce compliance-related risks and audit findings
- Enable efficient audit processes and evidence collection
- Maintain organizational compliance certifications and standing

### Typical Communication
- Compliance requirement reviews and risk assessments
- Audit reports and compliance evidence documentation
- Process approval for compliance-sensitive changes
- Regulatory update briefings and remediation tracking

### Cross-functional Interactions
- **With Project Managers**: Partners on process governance and compliance planning
- **With Security Stakeholder**: Aligns on security measures that support compliance requirements
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Coordinates on quality and testing requirements for compliance
- **With All Teams**: Communicates audit requirements and compliance obligations

---

## External Vendor/Partner Liaison

### Role Summary
External Vendor/Partner Liaisons manage relationships and integrations with third-party vendors and external partners. They ensure SLA compliance, coordinate deliverables, and act as the bridge between internal teams and external stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Manage ongoing relationships with external vendors and partners
- Coordinate third-party integrations and ensure technical compatibility
- Monitor and enforce Service Level Agreement (SLA) compliance
- Handle vendor escalations and performance issues
- Track vendor deliverables and timelines
- Communicate vendor status and risks to internal stakeholders

### Goals
- Maintain strong, collaborative relationships with external partners
- Ensure vendor deliverables meet quality and timeline expectations
- Minimize integration issues and vendor-related delays
- Maximize value from third-party relationships

### Typical Communication
- Vendor status reports and escalation tracking
- Integration coordination meetings with vendors and internal teams
- SLA compliance monitoring and performance reviews
- Vendor contract and relationship management documentation

### Cross-functional Interactions
- **With Project Managers**: Reports on vendor status, risks, and timelines; coordinates resource allocation
- **With Developers**: Coordinates technical integration requirements and troubleshooting
- **With Product Managers**: Ensures vendor solutions align with product requirements
- **With External Partners**: Acts as primary point of contact for vendor coordination

---

## Resource Manager

### Role Summary
Resource Managers optimize team capacity and skills allocation across projects. They manage resource planning, identify skill gaps, and have authority to adjust team composition and capacity allocation to meet project needs.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and allocate team capacity across multiple projects and initiatives
- Manage resource conflicts and priorities
- Identify skill gaps and plan workforce development or hiring
- Track team utilization and capacity metrics
- Adjust team composition based on project needs and constraints
- Coordinate with HR on recruitment and talent development

### Goals
- Optimize team capacity utilization across the organization
- Ensure projects have appropriate resources and skills
- Reduce resource conflicts and project delays due to staffing issues
- Support career development and retention of team members

### Typical Communication
- Capacity planning and resource allocation reviews
- Skill gap analysis and hiring planning
- Team utilization reports and forecasts
- Resource conflict resolution and prioritization meetings

### Cross-functional Interactions
- **With Project Managers**: Collaborates on resource requirements, allocation, and timeline planning
- **With Team Leads**: Assesses capacity, skills, and resource availability
- **With HR/Recruitment**: Coordinates hiring needs, onboarding, and talent development
- **With Executives**: Reports on organizational capacity and resource constraints
